About

The trainer behind the academy.

Acadia Canine Academy was built on one principle: dogs learn best when their handlers are consistent, clear, and committed.

Our Approach

Built from experience. Backed by results.

Based in Meteghan on Nova Scotia’s French Shore, we emphasize clear communication and consistent technique. Training is a discipline, not a shortcut, aimed at calm, focused, reliable dogs.

From foundational obedience to behaviour modification and rehabilitation for reactive and high-needs dogs, every program is rooted in clarity, fairness, and structure.

The Founder

Ryan R. McKenzie

Professional Dog Trainer · Canine Behaviour Consultant · Breeder · Educator

With over a decade of experience, Ryan works with families across the French Shore and beyond. His focus is foundational obedience, behaviour modification, and rehabilitation for reactive and high-needs dogs, delivered through responsible, humane training education.

He is also the author of four practical guides on puppy training, older dogs, general obedience, and canine rehabilitation, with two more on sport dogs and protection training coming soon.
