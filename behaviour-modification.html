Overview

Every dog is different. So is every plan.

Some dogs are struggling with more than manners. If your dog worries you, worries other people, or has become hard to live with, you are not alone, and it is not a reflection of how much you love your dog.

We offer Behaviour Modification after a consultation. We meet you and your dog first, listen to what is happening at home, and then build a plan that is personalized to your dog, your household and your goals. There is no one-size-fits-all program.

The kinds of issues we help with

  • Reactivity toward dogs or people
  • Fear, nervousness and shutting down
  • Guarding food, objects, places or people
  • Aggression

We will always be honest about what is realistic for your dog, and if a vet check or another professional would help, we will tell you.

Start with a free walk-in consultation.

Come meet us with your dog and tell us what is happening. We will talk through your options and whether Behaviour Modification is the right fit. We typically respond within 1 to 2 business days.
