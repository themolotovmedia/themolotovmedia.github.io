About the Academy

Built from experience. Backed by results.

Acadia Canine Academy was built on one principle: dogs learn best when their handlers are consistent, clear, and committed.

Based at 8204 Hwy 1 in Meteghan, in the Municipality of Clare, with an outdoor training field on the property and an indoor training space coming soon. Walk-in and consultation clients are welcome from Yarmouth, Digby and across southwest Nova Scotia, and in-home sessions cover the whole Municipality of Clare.
