FAQ

Dog training questions, answered.

Do you offer in-home dog training near Yarmouth or in Clare?

Yes, in Clare. In-home sessions cover Meteghan and the French Shore, at $75 CAD per hour with travel included. If you live farther away, for example in Yarmouth County, call or message to ask. Walk-in and consultation sessions at our place in Meteghan are $60 CAD per hour, and clients are welcome from Yarmouth, Digby and across southwest Nova Scotia.

How much does dog training cost?

Walk-in and consultation sessions at 8204 Hwy 1 in Meteghan are $60 CAD per hour. In-home sessions are $75 CAD per hour, travel included on the French Shore. Pricing for the full programs (Puppy Foundation, Basic Obedience, Advanced Obedience and Rehabilitation) will be posted when they launch.

What age should I start puppy training?

As soon as your puppy comes home. Our puppy course, Puppy Foundation, is built for puppies 8 to 16 weeks old, the window when habits form fastest. Older puppies and adult dogs can start with a consultation, or with Basic Obedience once it launches.

Do you work with reactive or aggressive dogs?

Yes. Our Rehabilitation program is for dogs that react to dogs or people, show fear, guard food or objects, or have become hard to manage. It starts with an honest assessment, puts safety and management first, and measures progress over time. No specific outcome can be guaranteed. A consultation is the place to start.

Ready to get started?

Contact us to discuss your dog’s needs. We typically respond within 1 to 2 business days.
